Geospatial Analysis

This page does the one thing no precedent GIS page manages: it says what the maps decided. Check each one against Entrepreneurship and Sustainability as well.

Facts that disagree between pages

A judge who reads two pages will notice these. Agree on one version as a team, then fix every page that carries the other.

  • Integrated Human PracticesWho is the target farmer now?The page doubts organic farmers as the target, then never says who replaced them. The Entrepreneurship page still sizes its obtainable market as about 4.7 million organic farms. Decide, then say it the same way on both pages.
  • Integrated Human PracticesExpansion countries differ between pagesThe BIO Asia entry lists the Philippines, Malaysia and the EU. The Entrepreneurship page says the same visit led you to the Philippines and Indonesia. The Laws page orders Taiwan, EU, Philippines, Vietnam, Thailand, Indonesia. Pick one list and use it on all three pages.
  • Integrated Human PracticesKill switch: yes or no?This page says you may drop the kill switch (Dr. Lin) and are still 'evaluating whether an additional kill switch is necessary' (Dr. Brophy). The Entrepreneurship page says the reactor has 'redundant genetic kill switches', including a CRISPR one. Decide what is true today and say it the same way on both pages.
  • Integrated Human PracticesWhat is the ink in his analogy?Here the ink is 'the biofertilizer'. A photo caption says 'the product'. The Entrepreneurship page says the ink is B. subtilis, swapped like a cartridge. ReLeaf is not called a biofertilizer anywhere else on the wiki. Use one meaning on every page.
  • Integrated Human PracticesTarget stresses differ across the wikiHere you narrowed to heat and salinity, and earlier you dropped osmotic stress after Prof. Cheng. The Laws page still claims AtLEA14 protects against drought and osmotic stress. Make the stress list match on every page.
  • Integrated Human PracticesWhich booth was surveyed?The Data Physicalization page says 62 of 116 visitors to its booth answered this survey. Here the same 62 stand for all six booths. Say which it was, and give the 116 if it applies.
  • Education“Promotion” sounds like marketingThe prize text asks for scientific learning and rules out marketing synthetic biology or iGEM. The word “promotion” works against you. Your Entrepreneurship page makes it worse: it calls educational outreach “Another one of our sales strategies”. Use “lessons” here, and ask the Entrepreneurship writers to reword their line.
  • EducationGame protectants differ from your projectCropshield names LEA peptides, NaD1 defensins and Bt toxins. Your Entrepreneurship page names LEA 14, ACCD and NaD1 in one place, and LEA proteins, ACCD and BoPep4 in another. Bt toxins appear on no other ReLeaf page. If the game uses a wider set on purpose, say so; if not, match the project.
  • EntrepreneurshipGeospatial page rates this risk higherYour risk table gives this risk Medium probability. The Geospatial Analysis page calculates that at NT$30,000 a unit “individual ownership is arithmetically out of reach for nearly the entire target sector”. Agree the rating with that team, and show your answer next to the risk.
  • EntrepreneurshipThird protein has two namesThe antifungal protein is NaD1 here and in Stage 1 of the funding plan, but BoPep4 in the Year 1 roadmap and in the summary at the end of section 6.1. The Laws and Regulations page uses only BoPep4. Pick one name for this page and say in one clause how it relates to the other, if they are related.
  • EntrepreneurshipFour different prices, no currencyThe number above this line is 135,297.77 with no currency. This sentence says 100,000 to 150,000, also with no currency. In the Our solution box you write “$60,000 NTD”, and the Geospatial Analysis page builds its whole cost model on “unit cost (NT$30,000)”. Agree on one price and one currency with the Geospatial team, then write it the same way in all four places.
  • EntrepreneurshipSDG numbers clash with SustainabilityThis page claims SDGs 2, 8, 11, 12, 13 and 15, and the SDG box lower down repeats them. The Sustainability page chooses SDG 2, 4, 10 and 15, and says plainly that Climate Action “isn’t the main point of our project”. Two pages cannot pick different goals. Use the Sustainability page's four and link to it.
  • EntrepreneurshipThe EU cannot be looser than EuropeThe sentence before this one lists the European Union as a first, easier market, then this sentence contrasts those markets with Europe. The roadmap also puts the EU last, in Year 5.5 and later, and the Laws and Regulations page gives the order as Taiwan, European Union, Philippines, Vietnam, Thailand, Indonesia. Three different orders exist across two pages. Agree one order and repeat it.
  • EntrepreneurshipLaws page calls this route unconfirmedYour Laws and Regulations page says it “did not identify a general Taiwan contained-use GMO framework that clearly resolves this specific engineered bacterial production scenario” and treats the status as unconfirmed. This page states the pathway as a fact and builds Phase 2 on it. Match the Laws page wording, or say what new source changed your view.

The six kinds of note

  • Praisewriting that already works, and why a judge rewards it
  • Restructurethe same facts in a clearer order
  • Cutwords the page does not need
  • Clarifyjargon, missing context, or knowledge the reader is assumed to have
  • Mistakeerrors, and facts that disagree with another page
  • Addwhat the judges' ballot asks for that the page does not yet give
