## Abstract
ReLEAF is an optogenetic bioreactor system that enables on-demand protection against combined biotic and abiotic crop stresses through cell-free delivery of protective proteins.
Our target market is conventional smallholder farmers; a single stress event can potentially wipe out an entire season’s income with no recovery mechanism or backup plan. The team is aware that not a lot of money can be made out of it, but our vision is to have a social impact, and to empower farmers, which is why we chose this business model instead of a traditional one. Our product is unlikely to be more effective than the current best chemical solutions; due to our product including GMOs, it will face legal hurdles.
