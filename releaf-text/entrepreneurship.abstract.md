## Abstract
ReLeaf is an optogenetic bioreactor system that enables automated, on-demand protection against combined biotic and abiotic crop stresses through cell-free delivery of protective proteins.
Our mission is to strengthen agricultural resilience by reducing crop losses while decreasing dependence on chemical interventions. Our vision is to empower every farmer to have their small bacteria factory for producing biostimulants. The team is aware that not a lot of money can be made out of it. Our product is unlikely to be more effective than the current best chemical solutions; due to our product including GMOs, it will face legal hurdles.
