## Abstract
Our education program focuses on helping students understand plant stress, climate change, sustainability, and synthetic biology through hands-on activities and interactive learning.
Rather than only talking about the advantages of synthetic biology, we included debates as part of the lesson itself. Across our four trials, we continuously adjusted and improved our workshop based on real-time feedback from students and teachers. DNA base-pairing remained the toughest concept to land. Teachers can easily integrate these materials into their existing curriculum without needing prior expertise in synthetic biology. All three teachers who took the survey indicated they would be willing to continue participating in or supporting the program in the future.
