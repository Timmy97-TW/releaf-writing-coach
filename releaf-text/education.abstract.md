## Abstract
Our education program focuses on helping students understand plant stress, climate change, sustainability, and synthetic biology through hands-on activities and interactive learning.
We designed the lessons and materials, built and prepared the educational activities, tested them through implementation in schools, and learned from survey results and feedback. Our program and lesson plan meaningfully improved students’ understanding of synthetic biology and plant science concepts, while also strengthening their attitudes toward science. DNA base pairing remained a challenging concept, with only 30% of students answering correctly after the lesson. All three teachers who took the survey indicated they would be willing to continue participating.
