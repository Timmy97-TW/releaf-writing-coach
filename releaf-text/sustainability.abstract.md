## Abstract
Sustainability is not only limited to environmental sustainability, but also the sustainability of our societal structure.
We talked with the leader of Ubuntu Social Enterprise Ghana; climate is actively harming their crops. We have an education website where we compiled all of the education resources from this year and previous years. Our culture medium turns agricultural and food-processing residues (hay, okara, papaya pulp and peels) into a growth medium for the bioreactor. Our product cannot reverse the damage done to the environment and soil directly. Due to the fact that our product always immediately supplies plants with the necessary protectants, it might make it so that the plants never actually grow their own resilience. This was mentioned by a natural farmer, Ms. Chen.
