## Abstract
Out of all the 17 SDGs available, we chose our SDGs first based on their relation to our project.
We ended up with SDGs 2, 4, 10, and 15, all of which are targets that we actively considered and either have or plan to enact in our project. We have an education website where we compiled all of the education resources from this year and previous years. While our product cannot reverse the damage done to the environment and soil directly, it can prevent further deterioration of its surrounding ecosystem. The usage of our product doesn’t promote longevity.
## Goals
- Zero hunger · SDG 2 · can directly impact this and increase food production
- Quality education · SDG 4 · basic introductions to synthetic biology
- Reduced inequalities · SDG 10 · subsidise small farmers’ purchases
- Life on land · SDG 15 · it can prevent further deterioration
- Negatives · Limit · doesn’t promote longevity
