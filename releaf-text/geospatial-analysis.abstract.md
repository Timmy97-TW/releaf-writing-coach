## Abstract
Our maps have shown that the land covering Taiwan’s small-scale farms, overlap with highest climate volatility, visualizing where our Bioreactor would help the most.
In Taiwan, 99.18% of farms are small-scale (under 2 hectares), yet together they work 86.18% of the island’s farmed land. For nearly a century, Taiwan’s average temperature crept upward by around 0.11ºC per decade; since 1991, that rate has nearly tripled to 0.29ºC per decade. We built a nationwide GIS routing model connecting every one of Taiwan’s 613 registered fertilizer companies to real farmland. This is the strongest argument for replacing the placeholder classification with parcel-resolved TARI soil survey data.
