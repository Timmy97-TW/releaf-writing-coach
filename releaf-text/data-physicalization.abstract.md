## Abstract
By creating the map and the sound physicalization, rather than presenting data through a graph, we invite the public to see, feel, and interact with patterns of environmental stress throughout Taiwan.
Paper color shows crop yield and flashing LEDs represent plant stress severity. Visitors were curious on why Yilan and Nantou had the highest stress. Many believed that regions such as Taipei would have the most stress instead. Most were fascinated or surprised by the fact that plants make clicking noises when they lack water. Just over half of visitors read the map correctly on a single, brief encounter. Next time, we will put a legend beside the board that shows three flash speeds and what each one means.
## Results
- Stress map · LEDs · More frequent flashing meant more severe stress
- Sound of plants · Headphones · recordings of drought-stressed tomato and tobacco
- Booth reach · Forum · 62 of the 116 people who visited our booth answered it
- Highest-stress region · Partial · 32 of 62 respondents (51.6%) correctly identified
- Willing to share · Survey · 60 of 62 participants (96.8%) said they were willing
