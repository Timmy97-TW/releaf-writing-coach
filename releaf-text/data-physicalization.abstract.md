## Abstract
We developed a data physicalization map that showcases different stress levels and crop yields throughout Taiwan.
We designed a physical map using styrofoam, colored paper, and LED lights to represent differences in crop yield and plant stress across different areas. We had 62 respondents and a total of 116 people who visited our booth. 59 of 62 participants (95.2%) agreed or strongly agreed that the Data Physicalization Map helped them understand the relationship between climate change and crop yield. Just over half of visitors read the map correctly on a single, brief encounter.
